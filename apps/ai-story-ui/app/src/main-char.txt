Use AI video tools:



* [Runway](https://runwayml.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

* [Luma Dream Machine](https://lumalabs.ai?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

* [Pika](https://pika.art?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Workflow



1. Generate Guddu full-body image.

2. Upload image to the AI video tool.

3. Enter a motion prompt.



Example:



```text

Guddu is walking through a beautiful park with a cute puppy.

Camera follows her slowly.

Her hair moves naturally in the wind.

She smiles and waves at the camera.

Cinematic animation.

```



The tool generates a 5–10 second animated clip.
